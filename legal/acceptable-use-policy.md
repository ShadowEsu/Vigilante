# Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective date:** June 15, 2026  
**Last updated:** June 15, 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy ("**AUP**") supplements the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) for **Vigilant, Inc.** ("**Vigilant**"). Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings in the Terms.

You must comply with this AUP when using the Vigilant Service. Violations may result in suspension or termination.

---

## 1. Permitted Uses

You may use Vigilant to monitor **publicly available** information for lawful business intelligence, competitive research, investment research, security awareness, and similar legitimate purposes, subject to applicable law and third-party rights.

---

## 2. Prohibited Uses

You may **not** use the Service to:

### 2.1 Unlawful or harmful activity

- violate any applicable law, regulation, or court order;
- stalk, harass, threaten, or dox individuals;
- discriminate or promote violence or hatred;
- engage in fraud, phishing, or social engineering.

### 2.2 Unauthorized access and interference

- access non-public, password-protected, or paywalled content without authorization;
- bypass robots.txt, rate limits, CAPTCHAs, or other technical controls;
- probe, scan, or test vulnerabilities of systems without written permission;
- interfere with or disrupt the Service or related infrastructure.

### 2.3 Intellectual property and contractual violations

- scrape or index content in violation of a website's terms of service where you lack legal authority;
- reproduce or redistribute third-party content at scale in violation of copyright or database rights;
- remove copyright or proprietary notices from indexed material.

### 2.4 Misuse of intelligence outputs

- present AI-generated briefs or signals as guaranteed fact without verification;
- use outputs as the sole basis for employment, credit, housing, or similarly regulated decisions about individuals;
- impersonate Vigilant or misrepresent the origin of reports.

### 2.5 Service abuse

- exceed plan limits through circumvention or multiple free accounts;
- resell, sublicense, or provide the Service to third parties without a written agreement;
- reverse engineer the Service except where permitted by law;
- upload malware or malicious code;
- send unsolicited bulk messages through notification features.

### 2.6 Sensitive and restricted targets

- monitor government classified systems, critical infrastructure control systems, or personal accounts without authorization;
- monitor minors or collect children's personal information;
- use the Service in jurisdictions or for entities subject to sanctions prohibiting such use.

---

## 3. Monitoring Targets

When adding watches, you are responsible for:

- confirming you have a lawful purpose and basis for monitoring each target;
- respecting website terms, copyright, and privacy expectations;
- not configuring watches on domains you do not own unless monitoring is clearly permitted.

We may block domains or URL patterns that pose legal, security, or reputational risk.

---

## 4. AI and Automated Actions

Do not configure downstream automations that cause unlawful or harmful effects based solely on AI output. You must review material signals before taking action that affects third parties.

---

## 5. Reporting Violations

Report suspected abuse to **legal@vigilant.io** with relevant URLs, account details, and description. We may investigate and cooperate with law enforcement where appropriate.

---

## 6. Enforcement

We may investigate violations, remove content or watches, throttle usage, suspend accounts, and terminate access. We are not obligated to monitor all activity but reserve the right to do so for compliance and security.

---

## 7. Changes

We may update this AUP by posting a revised version. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
