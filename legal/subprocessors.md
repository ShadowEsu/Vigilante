# Subprocessors

**Last updated:** June 15, 2026

**Vigilant, Inc.** uses the following third-party subprocessors to provide the Vigilant Service. This list may change; material updates will be reflected here and, where required, notified to customers under our [DPA](/legal/dpa).

| Subprocessor | Purpose | Location | Data processed |
|--------------|---------|----------|----------------|
| **Supabase, Inc.** | Authentication, PostgreSQL database, realtime | United States (region configurable) | Account emails, session tokens, Customer Data stored in the Service |
| **Vercel Inc.** | Web application hosting, edge delivery | United States / global edge | Request logs, IP addresses, application traffic |
| **Anthropic, PBC** | AI inference (change detection, briefs, signals) | United States | Excerpts of public page diffs and watch context; usage metadata |
| **Google LLC** | Optional search discovery; optional Gemini AI (Phase 2) | United States / global | Search queries, page excerpts when features enabled |
| **Stripe, Inc.** | Payment processing (when billing enabled) | United States | Billing name, email, payment method metadata, transaction records |
| **Resend / transactional email provider** | Magic-link auth and notification email | United States | Email addresses, message content |
| **Serper / Google Custom Search** (optional) | URL discovery for watch onboarding | United States | Company names/domains submitted for discovery |

---

## Notes

- **Customer-controlled integrations** (e.g., Slack webhooks you configure) are not Vigilant subprocessors; data flows directly per your integration settings.
- **Self-hosted / BYOK deployments** may reduce or replace certain subprocessors; enterprise agreements can document alternate infrastructure.
- Enterprise customers may request prior notice of subprocessor changes at **dpa@vigilant.io**.
