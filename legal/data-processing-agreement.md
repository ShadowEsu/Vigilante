# Data Processing Agreement

**Effective date:** June 15, 2026  
**Last updated:** June 15, 2026

This Data Processing Agreement ("**DPA**") forms part of the agreement between **Vigilant, Inc.** ("**Processor**" or "**Vigilant**") and the customer entity signing an order or using the Service ("**Controller**" or "**Customer**").

This DPA applies when Vigilant processes Personal Data on behalf of Customer in providing the Vigilant Service and where GDPR, UK GDPR, or similar data protection laws require a written processing agreement.

Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings in the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

---

## 1. Roles and Scope

**Customer** is the Controller of Personal Data it submits or instructs Vigilant to process (including account data, watch configuration, and notifications).

**Vigilant** is the Processor, except where Vigilant determines purposes and means of processing (e.g., account billing, security, product analytics), in which case Vigilant acts as an independent Controller for such limited purposes as described in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

**Processing** includes hosting, storage, retrieval of public web content on Customer instruction, change detection, AI analysis, notification delivery, and support.

**Duration:** For the term of the subscription plus deletion periods in Section 9.

---

## 2. Customer Instructions

Vigilant will process Personal Data only on documented instructions from Customer, including via Service configuration, unless required by law. If law requires processing beyond instructions, Vigilant will inform Customer unless prohibited.

Customer instructs Vigilant to:

- store and process account and configuration data to provide the Service;
- fetch and analyze publicly accessible URLs Customer configures;
- use subprocessors listed at [/legal/subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors);
- apply security measures in Section 6.

Customer is responsible for the lawfulness of instructions, including monitoring targets and notifications.

---

## 3. Confidentiality

Vigilant ensures personnel authorized to process Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations.

---

## 4. Subprocessors

Customer authorizes Vigilant to engage subprocessors. Current subprocessors are listed at [/legal/subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors).

Vigilant will:

- impose data protection obligations on subprocessors substantially similar to this DPA;
- remain liable for subprocessors' performance of obligations assigned to Vigilant under this DPA.

Vigilant will notify Customer of new subprocessors (email or in-product) and allow objection on reasonable grounds. If objection cannot be resolved, Customer may terminate affected Services.

---

## 5. Data Subject Rights

Vigilant will assist Customer, considering the nature of processing, in responding to data subject requests under applicable law, using appropriate technical measures. Customer may fulfill many requests via in-app tools or by contacting **privacy@vigilant.io**.

---

## 6. Security

Vigilant implements measures described in the [Security Overview](/legal/security), including:

- encryption in transit (TLS);
- access controls and authentication;
- logical separation of Customer data (including database row-level security);
- monitoring and incident response procedures.

Customer is responsible for securing its credentials and configuring watches lawfully.

---

## 7. Personal Data Breach

Vigilant will notify Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a Personal Data breach affecting Customer Personal Data, and provide information reasonably available to assist Customer in meeting breach notification obligations.

Notify security incidents to **security@vigilant.io**.

---

## 8. Data Protection Impact Assessments

Where required, Vigilant will provide reasonably available information to assist Customer with DPIAs or prior consultations, subject to confidentiality.

---

## 9. Deletion and Return

Upon termination, Vigilant will delete Customer Personal Data within **90 days**, except where retention is required by law or encrypted backups on normal rotation schedules. Customer may export data before termination where the Service provides export tools.

---

## 10. Audits

Upon reasonable written request no more than once per year (unless required by a supervisory authority), Vigilant will provide information necessary to demonstrate compliance, or allow audits under confidentiality and safety constraints. Customer bears audit costs unless material non-compliance is found.

---

## 11. International Transfers

Where Personal Data is transferred outside the EEA/UK, Vigilant uses appropriate safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses (Module Two: Controller to Processor) incorporated by reference, available upon request at **dpa@vigilant.io**.

---

## 12. CCPA/CPRA Service Provider Terms

To the extent CCPA/CPRA applies, Vigilant is a **Service Provider**. Vigilant will not:

- sell or share Personal Information;
- retain, use, or disclose Personal Information for any purpose other than providing the Service, as permitted by the CCPA/CPRA, or as required by law;
- combine Personal Information with other sources except as permitted for Service Providers.

---

## 13. Order of Precedence

If this DPA conflicts with the Terms regarding data protection, this DPA controls for Personal Data processing. Business-specific order forms may add details.
