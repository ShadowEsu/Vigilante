# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** June 15, 2026  
**Last updated:** June 15, 2026

**Vigilant, Inc.** ("**Vigilant**," "**we**," "**us**," or "**our**") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use the Vigilant target-intelligence platform, including our website, web app, APIs, and mobile applications (the "**Service**").

This Policy applies to visitors, account holders, and authorized users. If you use the Service on behalf of an organization, that organization may control certain Customer Data; this Policy describes our practices as a service provider.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information you provide

- **Account information:** email address and authentication credentials (magic-link sign-in).
- **Profile and settings:** notification preferences, scan schedules, integration settings, and billing details.
- **Watch configuration:** company names, domains, URLs, target types (company, person, ticker), source lists, budgets, and cadence settings.
- **Communications:** support requests, feedback, and correspondence with us.
- **Payment information:** processed by our payment provider; we receive limited billing metadata (not full card numbers).

### 1.2 Information collected automatically

- **Usage data:** pages viewed, features used, watch runs triggered, API calls, error logs, and performance metrics.
- **Device and browser data:** IP address, browser type, operating system, app version, language, and referral URLs.
- **Cookies and similar technologies:** see our [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies).

### 1.3 Information from public sources

When you configure watches, we retrieve and store **publicly available web content** from URLs you specify or that we discover, including page text, metadata, timestamps, and content hashes. This may include information about third parties (e.g., companies or individuals mentioned on public pages). We process this content to detect changes and generate intelligence outputs.

### 1.4 AI processing metadata

When we use AI models, we may send excerpts of page diffs and context to our AI providers and log token usage, model identifiers, and associated costs.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- provide, operate, and maintain the Service;
- authenticate users and secure accounts;
- run scheduled indexing, change detection, signal generation, and briefs;
- send notifications (email, and integrations you enable such as Slack when available);
- process payments and manage subscriptions;
- enforce our Terms and Acceptable Use Policy;
- comply with legal obligations;
- analyze usage to improve reliability, performance, and features;
- communicate about the Service, including security and product updates.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use Customer Data to train generalized AI models for unrelated products unless you opt in or we disclose otherwise in writing.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, comply with law, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Typical retention:

- **Account data:** until account deletion, plus a short backup period.
- **Snapshots and signals:** until you delete the associated watch or account, unless longer retention is configured on enterprise plans.
- **Usage and security logs:** generally up to 12–24 months.
- **Billing records:** as required for tax and accounting laws.

You may request deletion as described in Section 8.
