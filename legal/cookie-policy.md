# Cookie Policy

**Effective date:** June 15, 2026  
**Last updated:** June 15, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how **Vigilant, Inc.** ("**we**," "**us**") uses cookies and similar technologies on **vigilant.io** and related web properties (the "**Site**"), and in our progressive web application.

For how we handle personal data generally, see our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

---

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, and pixels. We use these to operate the Site, keep you signed in, remember preferences, and understand usage.

---

## 2. Types of Cookies We Use

### 2.1 Strictly necessary

Required for core functionality, such as:

- authentication and session management (Supabase auth cookies);
- security and fraud prevention;
- load balancing and basic performance.

These cannot be disabled without breaking the Service.

### 2.2 Functional

Remember settings such as UI preferences, notification choices, and demo/preview mode state.

### 2.3 Analytics (if enabled)

Help us understand how the Site is used (e.g., page views, feature adoption, error rates). We use analytics in a privacy-conscious manner and avoid unnecessary personal profiling where possible.

### 2.4 Marketing (if enabled)

We may use cookies for limited marketing measurement if we run advertising campaigns. We will request consent where required.

---

## 3. Third-Party Cookies

Our subprocessors may set cookies when you use the Site, including:

- **Supabase** — authentication and database client sessions;
- **Hosting provider (e.g., Vercel)** — delivery and performance;
- **Payment processor** — checkout and fraud prevention on billing pages.

See [/legal/subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors) for an updated list.

---

## 4. Mobile Applications

Our iOS and Android apps may use local storage and device identifiers for authentication tokens and app settings rather than browser cookies. Platform privacy disclosures in the App Store and Google Play describe app-specific data practices.

---

## 5. Your Choices

- **Browser controls:** Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Blocking necessary cookies may prevent sign-in.
- **Consent banner:** Where required (e.g., EEA/UK), we present a cookie consent mechanism for non-essential cookies.
- **Do Not Track:** We do not currently respond to DNT signals; we instead rely on applicable consent requirements.

To opt out of marketing emails, use the unsubscribe link in any message.

---

## 6. Retention

Session cookies expire when you close the browser. Persistent cookies remain until they expire or you delete them. Auth session duration follows your account security settings.
